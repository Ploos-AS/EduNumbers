# Exercises: historical and unusual bases

1. Convert `102₃` to decimal.
2. What is `10₁₂` in decimal?
3. What is `1A₁₂` if A represents digit value 10?
4. Convert `10₂₀` to decimal.
5. What is `Z₃₆` when A=10 and Z=35?
6. What is `10₃₆` in decimal?
7. Why does 1/2 have a terminating representation in bases 2, 10, and 12?
8. Explain why base 36 text does not require a “base-36 CPU”.
