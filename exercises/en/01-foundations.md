# Exercises: foundations

1. Write `13₁₀` in binary.
2. What is `101101₂` in decimal?
3. Convert `1111 0010₂` directly to hexadecimal.
4. What is `0x2A` in decimal?
5. How many different bit patterns exist with 4, 8, and 16 bits?
6. Explain in your own words why `10` can represent different values.
7. Write all powers of two from `2⁰` through `2¹⁶`.
8. Find the largest unsigned value representable with 8 bits.
