# Exercises: digital electronics

1. Write `0xA5` as eight bits and state which bit positions are 1.
2. Create a mask that sets bit 6 in an 8-bit register.
3. Create a mask that tests bits 2 and 5.
4. How many digital codes do 8-, 10-, and 12-bit ADCs have?
5. Use the idealized endpoint formula to estimate the voltage for code 512 in a 10-bit ADC with `Vref = 3.3 V`.
6. Why must HIGH and LOW thresholds come from the datasheet?
7. What does write-one-to-clear mean, and why can a naive read-modify-write be dangerous?
8. A logic analyzer shows `A5 02`. Name information needed before interpreting these bytes as a multibyte field.
