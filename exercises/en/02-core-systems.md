# Exercises: core number systems

1. Convert `11010110₂` to hex and decimal.
2. Convert `7B₁₆` to binary and decimal.
3. Convert `345₈` to binary and decimal.
4. Write 100₁₀ in binary, octal, and hex.
5. How many values exist in 12 bits?
6. What is the largest unsigned 12-bit value?
7. Explain why one hex digit corresponds to four bits.
8. Explain the difference between 4 KiB and 4 kB.
