# Suggested solutions: foundations

1. `13₁₀ = 1101₂`.
2. `101101₂ = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45₁₀`.
3. `1111 0010₂ = F2₁₆`.
4. `0x2A = 2 × 16 + 10 = 42`.
5. With `n` bits there are `2ⁿ` patterns: 16, 256, and 65,536.
6. The value of a position is determined by the base. `10_b = b`.
7. `1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768, 65536`.
8. `2⁸ - 1 = 255`.
