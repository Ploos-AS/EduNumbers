# Solutions: historical and unusual bases

1. `102₃ = 1×9 + 0×3 + 2 = 11`.
2. `10₁₂ = 12`.
3. `1A₁₂ = 1×12 + 10 = 22`.
4. `10₂₀ = 20`.
5. `Z₃₆ = 35`.
6. `10₃₆ = 36`.
7. A fraction terminates when the necessary prime factors of its reduced denominator occur in the base. Bases 2, 10, and 12 all contain factor 2.
8. Base 36 can be purely a textual representation. Software can convert between that text and an ordinary binary integer internally.
